Local voice dictation with tone rewrite

Record in the browser, transcribe on your own machine, and get the text back rewritten as casual, formal, technical, friendly or concise.

The problem

Dictation is faster than typing, but raw transcripts are full of filler and rarely match the tone the message needs. Cloud dictation tools also send audio off the machine, which rules them out for confidential work.

How it works

  1. Record in browser
  2. →
  3. Whisper transcribes locally
  4. →
  5. Local LLM rewrites in chosen tone
  6. →
  7. Copy to clipboard
  • Fully local. Speech recognition runs through faster-whisper on the CPU, and the rewrite runs through a local Ollama model.
  • Five tones. Casual, formal, technical, friendly and concise, each with its own rewrite instruction.
  • Fails safe. If the language model is unavailable, the app returns the raw transcript instead of an error. A health endpoint reports the status of both models.
